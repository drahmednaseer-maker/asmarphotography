Pakistan Spring Blossom Photography Tour

Apricot, cherry and almond trees in flower under peaks still deep in snow: the softest season in the Karakoram, and one of the shortest.

Enquire on WhatsApp Book via Travelbug ↗

For a few weeks in spring, the orchards of Gilgit-Baltistan flower all at once. White and pink blossom fills the terraces while the mountains above are still in full winter snow.

Blossom climbs the valleys with the warmth. It begins in the lower villages in late March and reaches Passu and the high villages of Baltistan around the middle of April, so a tour can follow it upward.

Spring is also the quiet season. The summer crowds have not arrived, the air is clean, and village life moves back into the fields, which makes it a good time for photographs with people in them.

Peak blossom
Late March to mid-April
Regions
Hunza, Nagar, Passu, Skardu, Khaplu, Machulu
Conditions
Cool, changeable, soft light
Access
Karakoram Highway; flights to Gilgit and Skardu

Locations

What you will photograph

  1. Lower Hunza and Nagar

    The first orchards to flower, usually in the last days of March.

  2. Karimabad and Altit

    Blossom on the terraces below the forts, with Rakaposhi behind.

  3. Passu

    The last of the Hunza blossom, around mid-April, in front of the Passu Cones.

  4. Skardu and Shigar

    Orchards around the Kachura lakes and Shigar Fort, in the first half of April.

  5. Khaplu and Machulu

    Whole villages in bloom along the Shyok River, outstanding from the air where drones are permitted.

Light and season

When to go

  • Overcast days are welcome: soft light holds the detail in white blossom.
  • A single day of wind or rain can strip the petals, so a few flexible days in the plan make a real difference.
  • Peak dates move by about a week from year to year with the temperature.

Practical notes

Before you travel

  • Babusar Pass is closed in spring, so travel is by the Karakoram Highway or by air.
  • Mornings are cold; some higher guesthouses are only just reopening.
  • A longer lens compresses blossom against the peaks; a wide lens works inside the orchards.
  • Orchards are private land. I arrange access with the families who own them.

Questions

Good to know

When is blossom season in Hunza?

From late March in lower Hunza to about mid-April in upper Hunza around Passu. The peak in any one village lasts roughly a week.

What kind of blossom is it?

Mostly apricot, along with cherry, almond, peach and apple. Hunza and Baltistan are known for their apricot orchards.

Is spring a good time to visit northern Pakistan?

Yes, for photographers. It is quiet, the light is soft and the snow line is still low, though the weather is more changeable than in autumn.

Can I combine blossom in Hunza and Skardu?

Yes. The two bloom within days of each other, and a longer itinerary can cover both by road through Gilgit.
