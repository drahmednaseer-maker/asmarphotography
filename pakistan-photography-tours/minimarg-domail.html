Minimarg & Domail Photography Tour

Beyond the Burzil Pass in Astore: a short summer season, emerald valleys, and Rainbow Lake at Domail.

Enquire on WhatsApp Book via Travelbug ↗

Minimarg and Domail lie in the far south of Astore district, over the Burzil Pass at about 4,100 metres. For most of the year the pass is under snow and the valleys are cut off.

In the few summer weeks when the road is open, this is some of the greenest country in northern Pakistan: pine forest, flower meadows, clear streams, and at Domail a lake that turns every shade of green and blue as the light changes.

This is a restricted area close to the Line of Control, and access is by prior permission only. That keeps visitor numbers very low. I only plan this route when permission can be confirmed in advance.

Best season
July to September
Altitude
About 2,850 m at Minimarg; about 4,100 m at the Burzil Pass
Base
Astore and Minimarg
Access
Jeep road from Astore over the Burzil Pass, by permission

Locations

What you will photograph

  1. Rainbow Lake, Domail

    A small lake surrounded by meadow and forest, at its best in still morning air.

  2. Minimarg village

    Wooden houses and fields in a green bowl below forested ridges.

  3. Burzil Pass

    A high pass on an old caravan route, with snow walls beside the road early in the season.

  4. Astore valley and Rama

    On the way in: Rama meadow and lake below the east face of Nanga Parbat.

Light and season

When to go

  • The season is short. July has snow on the pass and flowers below; September has clearer skies and the first autumn tones.
  • Calm mornings are essential for the lake; wind usually rises by late morning.

Practical notes

Before you travel

  • Permission is required for everyone and is not always granted, particularly for foreign nationals. I confirm it before any booking is made.
  • The road over the Burzil Pass is rough and weather-dependent.
  • Accommodation is basic. This is a route for photographers comfortable with simple conditions.
  • It combines well with Deosai, which is reached from the same Astore road.

Questions

Good to know

Where are Minimarg and Domail?

In Astore district, Gilgit-Baltistan, beyond the Burzil Pass, in the south of the region near the Line of Control.

Do I need a permit for Minimarg?

Yes. It is a restricted area and prior permission is required. Access for foreign nationals is subject to clearance and cannot be guaranteed.

When is the road open?

Roughly July to September, when the Burzil Pass is free of snow.

What is Rainbow Lake?

A small alpine lake at Domail, a short drive beyond Minimarg, known for the changing colours of its water.
