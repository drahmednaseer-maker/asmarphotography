The Best Photography Locations in Pakistan

Fifteen places I return to, from the Karakoram to the Arabian Sea, with the season that suits each one.

Enquire on WhatsApp Book via Travelbug ↗

Pakistan has five peaks above 8,000 metres, some of the longest glaciers outside the polar regions, a cold desert, a hot desert and a thousand kilometres of coast. For a landscape photographer it is close to inexhaustible.

This list is personal. These are the places I have photographed again and again over 18 years, and the ones I take other photographers to. Each links to a fuller guide where there is one.

The list

Fifteen places worth the journey

  1. Passu Cones, Upper Hunza

    The defining skyline of the Karakoram Highway, best at dusk. Best: Any season; dusk. Read the guide.

  2. Karimabad and Eagle's Nest, Hunza

    Rakaposhi, Ultar and Ladyfinger above terraced villages and two ancient forts. Best: October and April. Read the guide.

  3. Attabad Lake

    Turquoise water between bare rock walls, formed in 2010. Best: Spring to autumn; morning. Read the guide.

  4. Katpana Cold Desert, Skardu

    Sand dunes at over 2,200 m with snow peaks behind. Best: Sunrise, any season. Read the guide.

  5. Haldi Cones and Khaplu

    Granite spires over the Shyok River, far quieter than Passu. Best: April and October. Read the guide.

  6. Deosai Plains

    A plateau above 4,000 m with wildflowers, brown bears and dark skies. Best: July and August. Read the guide.

  7. Fairy Meadows

    A high meadow at about 3,300 m facing the north face of Nanga Parbat, 8,126 m, reached by jeep track and a hike. Best: June to September.

  8. Nagar and Hopar Glacier

    Rakaposhi and Diran close overhead, and a glacier beside the road. Best: October. Read the guide.

  9. Ghizer and Phander

    A river valley of poplars and still water west of Gilgit. Best: Mid-October. Read the guide.

  10. Broghil Valley, Chitral

    Remote high grassland and lakes near the Wakhan Corridor. Best: July to September. Read the guide.

  11. Kalash valleys and Chitral

    A distinct culture and the Hindu Kush under Tirich Mir. Best: May to October. Read the guide.

  12. Kumrat Valley

    Old deodar forest and a clear river in Upper Dir. Best: June to October. Read the guide.

  13. Rainbow Lake, Domail

    A small alpine lake beyond the Burzil Pass, open for a short summer. Best: July to September. Read the guide.

  14. Hingol and the Makran coast

    Badlands, mud volcanoes and empty beaches on the Arabian Sea. Best: November to February. Read the guide.

  15. Thar Desert, Sindh

    Dunes, villages and desert life in the far south-east. Best: November to February.

Questions

Good to know

What is the most photogenic place in Pakistan?

For mountain landscapes, most photographers would choose Hunza and the Passu Cones. Skardu offers the greatest variety, and Deosai the most space and wildlife.

Which region is best for a first photography trip to Pakistan?

Hunza. It is the easiest to reach and travel, with the highest density of viewpoints, and it combines well with Nagar and Passu.

Do I need a guide to photograph these places?

Many are accessible independently, but timing, permits, road conditions and access to private land make a real difference to the pictures. That is what a guided photography tour provides.

Can I buy prints of these locations?

Yes. Every photograph on this site is available as a signed archival print.
