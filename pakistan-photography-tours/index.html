Questions

Good to know

Does Asmar Hussain offer photography tours of Pakistan?

Yes. He leads private, tailor-made photography tours across northern Pakistan, including Hunza, Skardu, Deosai, the Karakoram, Broghil in Chitral and Fairy Meadows, with itineraries built around what you want to shoot and when you want to travel.

How much does a photography tour of Pakistan cost?

Every tour is privately quoted, because cost depends on the route, the number of days, group size and season. Share your dates and what you would like to photograph for a personalised quote.

When is the best time for a photography tour of Pakistan?

April to June for blossom and green valleys, and September to October for autumn colour in Hunza and the Karakoram. High areas such as Deosai and Broghil are usually accessible from June to September. The coast is best from November to February.

Do international visitors need a visa?

Most nationalities need a Pakistan tourist e-visa, applied for online before travel. Rules change, so always confirm the latest requirements on Pakistan's official online visa portal before booking.

Is northern Pakistan safe for photographers?

The main photography regions of Gilgit-Baltistan, including Hunza, Nagar, Skardu and Khaplu, and Chitral have strong safety records and are popular with domestic and international travellers. Local logistics, permits and access are arranged for every tour.

What camera gear do I need?

Any camera works, from a mirrorless or DSLR to a capable phone. A tripod plus wide and telephoto lenses help for landscapes. Drone use is possible in many areas subject to local permits.
