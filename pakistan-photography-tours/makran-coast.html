Makran Coast Photography Tour

Pakistan's other landscape: eroded badlands, empty beaches and the Arabian Sea along the Makran Coastal Highway to Gwadar.

Enquire on WhatsApp Book via Travelbug ↗

The Makran Coastal Highway runs about 650 kilometres from near Karachi to Gwadar, between a desert of eroded rock and the Arabian Sea. It is the opposite of the north, and just as photogenic.

I drove 5,000 kilometres round trip to photograph this coast, and wrote about it in a photo essay on the Makran Coastal Highway. The first half of the road crosses Hingol National Park, the largest in Pakistan, where wind and rain have carved the hills into spires and walls.

Beyond Hingol the highway meets the sea at Kund Malir and follows it west through Ormara and Pasni to Gwadar, where a hammerhead peninsula gives clifftop views over two bays.

Locations

What you will photograph

  1. Hingol National Park

    Fluted badlands, mud volcanoes and the rock formation known as the Princess of Hope.

  2. Kund Malir

    A long empty beach where the desert hills come down to turquoise water.

  3. Buzi Pass

    The highway winds through pale eroded cliffs; the classic road photograph of the trip.

  4. Ormara and Pasni

    Fishing towns with wide beaches, boats and soft evening light.

  5. Gwadar

    A hammerhead headland with sunset views from the cliffs, and Golden Beach nearby.

  6. Pishukan, Ganz and Jiwani

    Quiet coast west of Gwadar toward the Iranian border, with orange and white badlands meeting the sea.

Light and season

When to go

  • Shoot the badlands at sunrise and sunset; midday light flattens the texture.
  • Midday is best for the water, when the high sun turns the shallows turquoise.
  • Winter gives mild temperatures and clear air. Summer heat on this coast is severe.

Practical notes

Before you travel

  • Distances are long and services are far apart, so fuel, water and timing are planned carefully.
  • Travel arrangements in Balochistan follow current local guidance, and foreign visitors may need prior clearance.
  • A drone, where permitted, transforms this coast.
  • A tripod and neutral density filters open up long exposures of the sea.

Questions

Good to know

When is the best time to photograph the Makran coast?

November to February, when days are mild and the air is clear. Summers are extremely hot.

How long is the Makran Coastal Highway?

About 650 km from its start near Karachi to Gwadar, with Hingol National Park and Kund Malir in the first half.

What will I photograph on this route?

Desert badlands, mud volcanoes, long empty beaches, fishing harbours and clifftop sunsets over the Arabian Sea.

Can foreign visitors travel the Makran coast?

It is possible, but arrangements depend on current local guidance and may need advance clearance. This is confirmed before any booking.
