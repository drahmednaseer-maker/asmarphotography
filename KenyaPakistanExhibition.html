Exhibitors

The photographers

30 · Wildlife of Kenya · UH01–UH30

Dr. Usman ul Haq

Maxillofacial Surgeon & Wildlife Photographer

“We cannot conserve what we don’t know.”

Prof. Dr. Usman ul Haq is an Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon by profession and a nature lover by passion. He is an adventure scuba diver and off-road motorcyclist. He believes we cannot conserve what we don’t know about, so his photography aims to create awareness about birds and wildlife and help protect them. He has won multiple awards for his photographs and teaches medical and clinical photography at university.

20 · Landscapes of Pakistan · AH01–AH20

Asmar Hussain

Electronic Engineer & Sony Brand Ambassador

“Chasing Pakistan’s light: 18 years and counting.”

An electronic engineer and international award-winning photographer from Dera Ismail Khan, and Sony Brand Ambassador for Landscape Photography in Pakistan. For 18 years he has been capturing and promoting Pakistan, with work featured by National Geographic, TEDx, BBC World and Sony. He has exhibited nationally and internationally for 15 years.

10 · Landscapes of Pakistan · BK01–BK10

Bahram Khan

Professional Pilot & Photographer

“Pilot’s eye. Stories beyond the horizon.”

An Islamabad-based photographer whose work explores landscapes, wildlife, aviation and the human experience. With a pilot’s perspective and a documentary approach, he captures the extraordinary stories hidden within ordinary moments, celebrating the natural beauty and shared heritage of Pakistan and the world.
