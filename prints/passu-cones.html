Passu Cones

Passu, Hunza

The Passu Cones, also known as Tupopdan, rise to 6,106 metres above the Karakoram Highway in Upper Hunza. Their serrated skyline is the most recognisable in Pakistan, and it turns gold in the last hour of the day.
